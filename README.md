Super Starbie
Super Starbie is a small, interactive desktop pet. Its a Starbie but with customized to my liking.

## What Can Super Starbie Do?

- **Weather Updates:** Check the weather, temperature, and humidity.
- **Time and Date:** Keep track of the current time and date, add timers and reminders.
- **Have Expressions:** Display simple expressions that give it its own character.
- **Have motion-based interactions.**.
- **Utilize AI in some sort:** Haven't decided yet how to do this; I'll do it in the build week.

## Feautres

- **5 Mechanical Switches:** The project has multiple buttons placed around the PCB, making it easy to interact with the device and trigger different functions.

- **ESP32 Microcontroller:** The ESP32 acts as the brain of the project, handling the inputs, controlling the display, and providing wireless connectivity.

- **OLED Display:** The built-in display can show useful information, icons, and simple animations, making the device more interactive and easier to use.

- **Motion Sensing: ** The MPU6050 sensor detects movement and changes in orientation, allowing the device to react to physical movement.

- **Temperature and Humidity Sensor:** DHT11 measures the surrounding temperature and humidity, allowing the device to display them.
