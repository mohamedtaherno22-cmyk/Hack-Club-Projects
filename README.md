Super Starbie
Super Starbie is a small, interactive desktop pet. Its a Starbie but with customized to my liking.

## What Can Super Starbie Do?

- **Weather Updates:** Check the weather, temperature, and humidity.
- **Time and Date:** Keep track of the current time and date, add timers and reminders.
- **Have Expressions:** Display simple expressions that give it its own character.
- **Have motion-based interactions.**.
- **Utilize AI in some sort:** Haven't decided yet how to do this; I'll do it in the build week.
