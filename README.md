# Introduction to Super Starbie

Super Starbie is a small, interactive desktop companion shaped like a little star. I wanted to create something more than just another electronic device — something fun, useful, and with a personality of its own. The goal is to combine electronics, programming, and a bit of creativity to build a companion that can interact with me and make everyday tasks more enjoyable.

At its core, Super Starbie is built around a custom star-shaped PCB that brings together different components, including an ESP32, an OLED display, physical buttons, and an MPU6050 motion sensor. Each part has its own purpose, allowing Starbie to display useful information, respond to button presses, and detect movement.

## What Can Super Starbie Do?

Super Starbie is designed to make everyday information and interactions simple and enjoyable.

- **Weather Updates:** Check the weather, temperature, and humidity.
- **Time and Date:** Keep track of the current time and date, with the possibility of adding timers and reminders.
- **Physical Buttons:** Use the buttons on Starbie's legs to access different functions, with LEDs providing visual feedback.
- **OLED Expressions:** Display icons, animations, and simple expressions that give Starbie its own character.
- **Motion Detection:** Use the MPU6050 gyroscope and accelerometer to detect movement and orientation, opening up possibilities for motion-based interactions.
- **AI Interaction:** Connect Starbie to a PC to enable smarter interactions and conversational features without needing powerful hardware inside the device itself.

## The Idea Behind the Project

This project is also an opportunity for me to learn more about PCB design,  programming, sensors, ... 

There is still plenty of room to improve and add new features, but the long-term goal is to make Super Starbie feel less like an ordinary gadget and more like a little robotic friend.
